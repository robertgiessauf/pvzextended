Tool: built-in imagegen. All outputs requested a transparent background.

Design prompt:
A new Plants vs. Zombies-inspired PlanternNut in the original game's chunky,
hand-painted cartoon style: a sturdy walnut whose protective shell has grown
into a living lantern. A honey-amber frosted chamber in its belly is protected
by thick, curved walnut-shell ribs. Small glowing fissures are part of the
shell. Friendly eyes and a smile occupy the upper shell, with a green sprout
and small base leaves. Keep the silhouette readable at approximately 70 pixels.
Use dark brown outlines. No separate pasted lamp, metal accessories, arms,
background, text, or cast shadow. Crisp silhouette and genuine transparency.

Sprite-sheet prompt (concept.png used as reference):
Create a three-column, two-row sheet of this same character. Top row: healthy,
moderately damaged, heavily damaged, all with open eyes. Bottom row: the same
three damage states with fully closed blinking eyes. Preserve identity, colors,
proportions, pose, placement, and scale across every cell. Transparent background,
no labels, backgrounds, or shadows.

Transparency refinement prompt (sprite sheet used as reference):
Remove only the colored background and outer halo. Preserve the exact six
sprites, layout, colors, proportions, pose, and damage states. Empty background
must be genuinely transparent with alpha zero. Do not redesign the artwork.

Files:
concept.png: original generated character design.
sprite-source.png: full-resolution generated sprite sheet after transparency refinement.
preview.png: six game sprites shown on a lawn-colored background.
Debug/reanim/PlanternNut_*.png: mechanically cropped and resized 100x100 RGBA sprites.
build-animation.ps1: rebuilds the editable and compiled reanimation from the
Wall-nut idle motion and the new sprite resource names. Run using PowerShell.
prepare-sprites.ps1: crops and resizes sprite-source.png using the same alignment
for all six sprites and produces the game sprites and preview. Run using PowerShell.
